About Graceland PVO

Compassion, integrity and community partnerships that transform lives

Graceland Private Voluntary Organization (PVO) is a registered humanitarian organisation committed to complementing Government efforts to alleviate poverty and improve the standards of living of vulnerable communities. We work with compassion, integrity, and community partnerships to provide sustainable solutions that transform lives.

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable individuals and families through humanitarian assistance, healthcare, education, community empowerment, and sustainable development programmes.

Our Vision

To build empowered, healthy, self-reliant communities where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, and purpose.
